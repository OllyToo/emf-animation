EMF & Internal Resistance
ε = I ( R + r )
Click a variable below to explore · Use sliders to see the relationship
ε
EMF — Electromotive Force
The total energy supplied per unit charge by the source. Measured in Volts (V). It is NOT a force — it's the work done per coulomb moving charge around the whole circuit, including inside the cell.
I
Current
The rate of flow of charge through the circuit. Measured in Amperes (A). The same current flows through both R and r (series circuit), so I multiplies the total resistance.
R
External Resistance
The resistance outside the cell — e.g. a lamp or resistor. Measured in Ohms (Ω). The voltage across R is the terminal p.d. = IR, and is always less than ε.
r
Internal Resistance
Resistance inside the cell itself. Measured in Ohms (Ω). Causes a "lost volts" drop of Ir. A higher r means more energy wasted as heat inside the cell — common in old batteries!
